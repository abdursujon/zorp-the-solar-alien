1. Collect facts from different points in a planet
2. Answer some question about what fact they learnt, if not correct answer a meteor hits their body losing half the health 
3. If answer correctly, meteor destroyed and get some score. 
4. Has to reach certain score to reach the next level 
5. Total points to collect is 1000, and each point of visit gives 100, there are 12 area to visit to get 1000.
6. When answer correctly get 50+ and wrong -50. 

How the game level will be design 
1. Parallax background — layered scrolling images + procedural particles (theme per level)
2. Player (Zorp) — move with arrow keys
3. Player shooting — fires projectiles (ranged attack)
4. Player melee — close range attack
5. Enemies — spawn, random movement, contact damage (type/skin per level)
6. Enemy shooting — enemies fire projectiles at player
7. Health system — player HP, damage from enemy contact + enemy projectiles
8. Collision detection — player projectile vs enemy, enemy projectile vs player, enemy vs player, player vs fact points
9. Fact points — 12 collectibles with facts about the current planet
10. Score display — score + collected count (X/12)
11. Audio — background music + sound effects (theme per level)
12. Level complete — 10+ facts collected → unlock next planet
